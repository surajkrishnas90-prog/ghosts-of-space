### Image identifier and attribution verification

- **AS14-67-9361** is the Apollo 14 film frame identifier. NASA's old Image and Video Library file slug is zero-padded as `as14-67-09361`; that archive slug does not change the frame ID printed in the original mission photographic records. Credit the frame as **NASA/JSC**. [NASA Apollo 14 photo index](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/alsj/a14/a14.photidx.pdf); [NASA public lunar site report](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/617743main_nasa-usg_lunar_historic_sites_reva-508.pdf).
- **PIA00623** is a single Pathfinder IMP image showing Sojourner after it drove down the lander ramp; it is not a touchdown animation/sequence. Credit: **NASA/JPL**. [NASA Photojournal caption](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/rover-touchdown-on-martian-surface/).
- **PIA00662** is the 360-degree black-and-white “Monster Pan,” taken by the Pathfinder IMP on Sol 3; deployed Sojourner and landing hardware are visible. Credit: **NASA/JPL**. [NASA Photojournal caption](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/360-degree-bw-monster-pan/).
- **PIA10701** credit is **NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona** (enhanced-color HiRISE image). **PIA25287** credit is **NASA/JPL-Caltech**. [NASA Photojournal Phoenix catalog](https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/mission/Phoenix); [NASA Photojournal InSight final selfie](https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA25287).
- NASA's current Surveyor 3 mission page gives 00:04:17 UT on April 20, while the NASA NSSDC Surveyor summary gives 00:04:53 UT. Both agree on April 20 shortly after 00:04 UT; this site intentionally uses only the minute-level time rather than selecting between conflicting second-level records. [NASA Surveyor 3](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/surveyor-3/); [NASA NSSDC Surveyor summary](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/lunar/surveyor.html).
- The InSight audio button is explicitly labeled “Play illustrative seismic tone”; adjacent copy states it is original exhibit sound design, not a recording of S0235b or Mars seismic audio. No audio effect is presented as an authentic recording.

## Performance and QA notes

- All eight photo files are valid JPEGs downloaded from NASA's official assets host; they were resized/compressed for web use.
- Image dimensions were checked from the actual local files. Sojourner PIA00623 is 471 × 285 px and Phoenix PIA10701 is 508 × 600 px; both are low-resolution sources and should be reserved for cards/insets rather than full-screen backgrounds.
- All three downloaded GLBs parse as valid GLB 2.0 files and contain embedded meshes. Local sizes: Moon 13,799,876 bytes; Mars 4,032,148 bytes; Apollo Lunar Module 716,840 bytes.
- Each image and model filename is recorded in this register and resolves to its expected local file.
- The approved NASA Moon GLB remains 13.8 MB; the homepage uses an optimized 1.63 MB derivative with a 506 KB external 2048px texture. The Apollo/Mars globe models are about 0.7/4.0 MB. The homepage model is rendered with the locally stored `model-viewer` runtime, and its auto-rotation is disabled for reduced-motion users.
- The separate 11.9 MB Mars Exploration Rover model was identified but omitted because it depicts Spirit/Opportunity rather than the chosen Pathfinder/Phoenix/InSight artifacts; do not ship irrelevant model bytes.
- The React + Vite homepage is implemented; Moon, Mars, artifact browsing, and timeline pages are not built yet. The exact direct dependencies are recorded in `package.json`.
- `work/vendor/model-viewer.min.js` is retained for the temporary preview gallery; the same local runtime is copied to `public/vendor/model-viewer.min.js` for the homepage. The included license notice is `work/vendor/MODEL-VIEWER-LICENSE.txt`.
