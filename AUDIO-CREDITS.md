# Audio Credits and Usage Notes

## Included media files

There are currently no bundled audio files in `public/audio/`. No external streaming audio is used.

## Original synthesized sound design

The ambient layer, chapter tones, transition sweeps, interface cues, Apollo laser cue, and illustrative InSight seismic tone are synthesized in the browser by `src/audio/soundEngine.js` using the Web Audio API. They are original educational sound design, not recordings of space, the Moon, Mars, the LR-3 experiment, or the S0235b event. They are not derived from NASA audio or NASA data. No third-party audio license or attribution applies to these generated waveforms.

## NASA recording policy

No NASA audio recordings are included. NASA’s media guidance says NASA media is generally available for factual educational/informational use with NASA acknowledged as source, while noting that some material on NASA sites may be third-party copyrighted: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/ . The synthesized Mars wind-inspired texture and seismic tone must not be described as actual Martian recordings.
