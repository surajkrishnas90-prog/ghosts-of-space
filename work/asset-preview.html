Known collection limitations

  • PIA00623 is only 471 × 285 pixels in the downloaded NASA medium variant; suitable for a small card or inset, not a hero image.
  • Phoenix PIA10701 is 508 × 600 pixels in NASA's available original; it is also a small card/detail image, not a hero background.
  • The PIA00662 panorama is very wide (1800 × 319 after optimization); it can look short in a card. Original NASA panorama available from its source page.
  • No artifact-specific GLB was confirmed for the LR-3, Surveyor 3, LRV, ALSEP, Pathfinder, Sojourner, Phoenix, or InSight. Moon/Mars globes and the Lunar Module are identified as context models.
  • No dedicated lunar or Mars section-background image, audio, or icon pack was downloaded.
  • GLB files are local and interactive; the Moon model is about 13.8 MB and may take longer to load on slower devices.
